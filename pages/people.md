The “People” page provides brief profiles of enslaved and free African-descended and indigenous people in the documents. 


The goal of these profiles is to provide a more detailed narrative description of persons who, on many occasions, receive scant or problematic representation in the documents. In addition to making note of these named and unnamed historical actors, the profiles also detail connections between individuals – familial ties, social ties, etc. Even unnamed individuals are documented to acknowledge the role they played in either the events described in the colonial documents or the lives of the people described in them.
