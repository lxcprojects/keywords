is a recent graduate of Johns Hopkins University where they received a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology with concentrations in Visual Arts and Spanish. They are the Assistant Editor of the Keywords for Black Louisiana Spanish Language Team as well as a Research Assistant for Black Archives, Education and History in Louisiana (BAEHLa). Their research interests include uncovering the livelihoods of African ancestors hidden as a result of the Transatlantic slave trade, decolonial psychology, and the impacts of developmental & systemic trauma on the well-being of youth and adult populations. They work as a mental health counselor for adolescents with histories of trauma.
